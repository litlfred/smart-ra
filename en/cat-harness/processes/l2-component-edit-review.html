L2 component edit and review

Process_L2ComponentEditReview · advisory · 6 step(s)

One pass over one L2 DAK component: the agent edits it, runs its checker, renders and stages it with a before/after, and hands it on; review forks off beside that hand-on and never holds it up (owner, 2026-10-14: adjudication is QA, not a gate). Called by each authoring track of l2-dak-authoring.bpmn (Process_L2Dak), one call per component pass.

The call’s inputs, which are the only things that differ between components (skill l2-component-edit-review, “The inputs, per component”):

  • component: personas and user scenarios (C2, C3), business processes (C4), core data elements and glossary (C5) with their terminology bindings, decision-support and scheduling logic (C6, C7), indicators and requirements (C8, C9);
  • authoring skill: l2-dak-authoring, bpmn-authoring, dmn-authoring or terminology-management, named on the calling activity, so it joins this process’s skills along the call path (review-task.bpmn’s scoped composition);
  • renderer and diff: what the reviewer looks at, before and after;
  • checker: the mechanical checks the agent runs before asking anyone;
  • reviewing role: the person who takes on this process’s reviewer lane for the call.

One process rather than one per component because nothing but those inputs differs: DMN’s test cases are its checker, BPMN’s picture is its renderer, the glossary’s term diff (dak-data-dictionary-diff.ts) is its diff and its binding check its checker, and traceability to L1 is the checker of personas, scenarios and requirements.

Lanes — who acts

lane role what it does here
Authoring agent authoring-agent Does everything except judge. Edits the component with the authoring skill the call names, runs the component’s checker, renders it and makes the before/after preview on a staged build, hands the component on without waiting, and asks for review with staging deep links. Prepares the review material and never records the verdict (smart-guideline-end-to-end, rule 4). On request-changes it edits again; on reject it reopens the stage upstream and flags what was derived from it.
Component reviewer (person) reviewer A person, never the agent, in the role the call names for this component: the clinical SME for business processes, decision logic, personas and scenarios; the terminologist for terminology bindings and the glossary’s codes, since the person who chose a code does not review it; the business analyst with the programme manager for indicators and requirements. Each takes on this generic reviewer lane for the call path only (review-task.bpmn), so the reviewer of a DMN table does not statically carry the terminologist’s skills. Reads the staged component, its before/after and its checker’s findings; the preview is made in the agent’s lane and only read here.
