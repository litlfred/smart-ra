Questionnaire wireframe review

Process_QuestionnaireWireframeReview · advisory · 10 step(s)

A built FHIR Questionnaire shown the way a person will meet it — a mid-fidelity wireframe at web and mobile width, and a live LHC-Forms form view — checked field for field against the L2 wireframe it was derived from, reviewed as a design, staged with deep links, and changed through requests that say which side was edited. Operating skill: fhir-questionnaire-wireframe. Owner decisions, 2026-10-14: LHC-Forms, vendored; the checker lives in this layer; either the Questionnaire or the wireframe may be the source of a change, and the request says which.

WHERE IT SITS. After SUSHI has compiled the Questionnaire to JSON (l3-fhir-pipeline, Task_Sushi), and wherever a layer above reviews a Questionnaire’s fidelity to its wireframe. It reads JSON only, so it can run on any IG that produces a Questionnaire.

WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM. A faithful Questionnaire is not therefore a good form, and an accepted form is not therefore conformant: wireframe-design-review judges the first and fhir-validation the second.

Lanes — who acts

lane role what it does here
FHIR modeller fhir-modeller Owns the Questionnaire end to end: reads the BUILT JSON, draws the wireframe and the form view, asks for review with deep links, triages the change requests that come back and applies each to the side the request did not touch, so the Questionnaire and its L2 wireframe agree again. It is also the only lane with a path back to the start: every accepted change re-enters at Task_Read, so a change is rendered, checked and reviewed exactly as the first version was.
Build pipeline — rendering, screenshots, fidelity build-pipeline The mechanical steps, none of which decides anything a person has to own: screenshots at both viewports (wireframe-check), the field-for-field fidelity check against the L2 wireframe when there is one, and the staging build. A fidelity finding does not stop the run here; it travels into the review as evidence, because whether a mismatch is the wireframe’s fault or the Questionnaire’s is the change request’s call, not the checker’s.
Business analyst — design review business-analyst Reviews the rendered form as a design against the workflow it serves, through wireframe-design-review: blind, per criterion, at web and mobile width, adjudicated where reviewers disagree. Here because the question is whether the form lets its user do the task, which is the business analyst’s to answer rather than the modeller’s, who drew it.
Clinical reviewer — the staged form clinical-sme Reads the staged form view and wireframe at the deep links Task_AskReview sent, and judges what the form asks and in what order. The only lane that can send the Questionnaire back: a change is requested through ✍ suggest or the questionnaire-change issue form, on either side, and nothing else re-opens the loop.
