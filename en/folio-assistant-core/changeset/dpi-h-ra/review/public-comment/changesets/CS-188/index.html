title
Bound the Benefits Package Registry against scheme operations
requirements
- Distinguish the Benefits Package Registry and other registries from enrolment, contracting, claims and payment systems - Define the BPR as the authoritative publication of benefit definitions, not an eligibility or adjudication engine - Limit the registry to authoritative benefit definitions and machine-readable rules - Require CBMS implementations to reconcile enrolment against the shared Client Registry identity - Separate workforce credential checks from contracting, empanelment and claim validity - Separate payment rails from decisions about correctness and authorisation; use measured language about fraud prevention - Keep enrolment, empanelment and contracting in operational systems that reference registries - State that enrolment, eligibility determination, contracting and adjudication sit in operational systems
