comment
All four offline rows say they are meant for remote and low-resource areas, yet all four are only Recommended. In real procurement, anything marked Recommended is usually dropped to save cost, so the feature that matters most for far-flung areas is the easiest one to skip. But making all four Required is also a problem: it would force offline support even on well-connected hospitals that do not need it, and could make the standard too hard to meet for the small local vendors who serve remote areas. Status and setting are being squeezed into one value.
