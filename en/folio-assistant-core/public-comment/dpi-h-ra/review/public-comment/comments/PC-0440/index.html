The draft provides useful lifecycle governance for semantic artefacts and recommends continuing national conformance monitoring. However, it does not yet make explicit how a product-level certification result remains interpretable as the normative baseline evolves. Without an explicit binding to the versions tested, a product may remain labelled “certified” even after relevant profiles, terminology resources, test suites or product releases have materially changed.
