Note: The three components above are complementary, not competing. A functioning health financing system requires all three. The Benefits Package Registry provides the what; the Contract and Benefits Management System provides the operational how; and the Beneficiary Registry provides the who. Conflating them — most commonly by building enrolment and operational management directly into the package registry, or by including package definitions inside a scheme-specific system — produces fragmentation, duplication, and the inability to see the national coverage picture as a whole.
