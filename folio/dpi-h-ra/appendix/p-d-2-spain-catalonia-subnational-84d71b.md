The Catalan health service operates a mature shared clinical history (**HC3**) that aggregates primary care, hospital, mental health, pharmacy and social-care data into a unified record with a patient-facing access channel \cite{jmir-2024-e58933}, alongside a health-system performance observatory and an epidemiological surveillance system that integrates environmental and climate-related health monitoring \cite{salut-publica-catalunya}. It shows how a subnational authority can operate sophisticated, multi-function capability within a multilevel governance architecture, including the integration of social determinants and climate-related surveillance.
