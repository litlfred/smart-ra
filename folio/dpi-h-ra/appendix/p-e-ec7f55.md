**What the health system needs to be able to do:** the system needs to resolve each person to a single, verifiable identity and to assemble their longitudinal record so that it can be reached wherever care is given. It needs to let people see and govern who accesses their information, through consent that can be granted, recorded, and withdrawn. And it needs to assure the authenticity and integrity of records and of the people and institutions that contribute to them, so that a record can be trusted by a provider who did not create it.
