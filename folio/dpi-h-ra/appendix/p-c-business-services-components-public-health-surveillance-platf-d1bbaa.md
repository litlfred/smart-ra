Requirements draw on the WHO Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR) technical guidelines (3rd edition, 2019)[^64], the International Health Regulations 2005 reporting framework[^65], the WHO Event Information System (EIS) operational standards[^66], the WHO Integrated Data Platform specifications[^67], and published evidence from national eIDSR implementations. There is no direct equivalent of the PHSP in the OpenHIE architecture specification.
