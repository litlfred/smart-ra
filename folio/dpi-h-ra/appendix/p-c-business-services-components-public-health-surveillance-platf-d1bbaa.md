Requirements draw on the WHO Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR) technical guidelines (3rd edition, 2019) \cite{who-afro-2019-idsr-guidelines}, the International Health Regulations 2005 reporting framework \cite{9789241580496-eng}, the WHO Event Information System (EIS) operational standards \cite{who-eis-operational-standards}, the WHO Integrated Data Platform specifications \cite{who-integrated-data-platform}, and published evidence from national eIDSR implementations. There is no direct equivalent of the PHSP in the OpenHIE architecture specification.
