**Note:** *These requirements are consistent with and extend the* *OpenHIE* *SHR specification (*[*https://guides.ohie.org/arch-spec/openhie-component-specifications-1/openhie-shared-health-record-shr*](https://guides.ohie.org/arch-spec/openhie-component-specifications-1/openhie-shared-health-record-shr) *). The DPI-H reference architecture extends* *OpenHIE* *in five principal respects: (1) the term Lifelong Health Record replaces Shared Health Record to reflect longitudinal and federated scope; (2) consent management integration is elevated to Required; (3) federated and distributed architecture support is introduced as an explicit requirement; (4) individual access and portability is elevated to Required; and (5) data immutability and versioning is elevated to Required. Several* *OpenHIE* *Recommended requirements have been elevated to* *Required* *to reflect the clinical safety and governance context of a nationally governed LHR.*
