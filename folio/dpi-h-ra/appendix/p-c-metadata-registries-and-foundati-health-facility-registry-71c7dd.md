Where a functioning CGR is not available at the foundational level — as is the case in many countries today — it is important that the Health Facility Registry manages its geographic reference data internally, accepting the limitations this creates for multi-system geographic consistency. This is a common practical situation that the reference architecture acknowledges, as in many cases, the health sector is unable to wait for foundational geospatial DPI to be fully developed, but its implementations should be designed with the expectation of eventual integration with a CGR as that foundational layer matures.
