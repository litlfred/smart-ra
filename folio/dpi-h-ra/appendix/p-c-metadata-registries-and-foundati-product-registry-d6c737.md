The Product Registry is one of the Core Registries for Health within the DPI-H reference architecture. Its closest structural counterpart is the Product Catalogue Management Tool, which extends the registry with health-specific operational attributes — dosage guidance, substitution rules, adverse event linkage, procurement pricing, and clinical interaction data — that are too domain-specific and dynamically changing to belong in the foundational registry layer. The Product Catalogue Management Tool consumes the Product Registry as its master source: all items in the catalogue it produces should trace to a registry record, and the registry's identifiers are the canonical keys used throughout the supply chain.
