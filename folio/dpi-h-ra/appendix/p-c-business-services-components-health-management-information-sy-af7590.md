The **Logical Information Model Repository** **(LIMR)** provides the structural definitions that govern how data ingested by the HMIS is organised and interpreted. Consistent data structures across contributing systems are a prerequisite for reliable indicator aggregation; the LIMR ensures that the data the HMIS receives conforms to the logical models against which its indicator calculations are defined, and that changes to those models are managed in a coordinated way rather than introducing silent inconsistencies into aggregate outputs.
