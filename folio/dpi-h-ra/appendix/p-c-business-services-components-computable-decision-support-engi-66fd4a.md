The CDSE is structured around three interdependent elements that parallel the components of any decision support system. The knowledge base — sourced from and governed through the LIMR — provides the computable rules, guidelines, algorithms, and care pathway logic that the engine evaluates. The inference engine is the CDSE itself: the computational service that retrieves patient and contextual data, evaluates it against the applicable rules, and produces a structured output. The communication mechanism is the interface through which the output is delivered to the consuming system; in standards-based implementations this is expressed through CDS Hooks or SMART on FHIR integration patterns embedded within point-of-care clinical applications.
