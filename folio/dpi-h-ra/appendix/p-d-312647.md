This appendix records selected national, subnational and regional implementations, together with the standards-based tools and technologies on which they draw, to illustrate approaches that countries have taken in building the shared digital resources described in this guidance. Each example is intended to help ministries of health, digital public infrastructure authorities and implementation teams recognise patterns relevant to their own setting, and to see how the components articulated in Section 3.5 and Appendix C have been realised in practice across a range of health system contexts and levels of maturity.
