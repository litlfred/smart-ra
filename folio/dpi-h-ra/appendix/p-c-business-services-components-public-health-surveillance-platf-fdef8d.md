The Public Health Surveillance Platform is a governed, shared application component for the systematic, ongoing collection, analysis, interpretation, and dissemination of health data to detect, monitor, and respond to public health threats at local, national, and international levels. It supports both routine indicator-based surveillance, aggregated from health facility reporting and the Lifelong Health Record, and event-based surveillance, capturing signals from community reports, laboratory alerts, environmental monitoring, and other non-routine sources. Together, these modalities provide the analytical and operational foundation for early warning, outbreak detection, case investigation, and coordinated public health response.
