The Lifelong Health Record is the CDSE's primary source of person-specific clinical data. At the moment a decision support request is triggered — typically by a clinical action in a point-of-care system — the CDSE queries and retrieves the relevant clinical context from the LHR: the patient's active diagnoses, current medications, allergies, recent observations, and care history. The CDSE does not hold its own copy of person-level clinical data; it accesses the LHR in context and at the moment of decision. Clinical actions that result from CDSE guidance are recorded back into the LHR by the point-of-care system, closing the loop between decision support and the longitudinal record.
