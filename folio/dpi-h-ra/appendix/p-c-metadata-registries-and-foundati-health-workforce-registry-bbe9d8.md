Other considerations are that patients want to be assured that they are receiving care from practitioners whose registration status or licence can be verified at the point of service, health system managers want to know that fraud in payroll and insurance claims can be detected when provider identity can be confirmed, and ministries of health want to be able to make workforce planning decisions on reliable data, to address shortages and ensure distribution of the health workforce relative to population need. The WHO reports that the lack of sufficient health workers was consistently the most common cause of disruptions to essential health services, including during health emergencies like COVID-19 — a finding that depends on the kind of reliable workforce data that a functioning registry can provide (WHO NHWA handbook, Second Edition, 2023).
