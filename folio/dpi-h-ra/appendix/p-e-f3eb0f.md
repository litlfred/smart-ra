**What the health system needs to be able to do:** the system needs to know who its health workers are and what each is qualified and authorised to do, holding an authoritative record of identity, occupational classification, professional registration, and licensing status that clinical, administrative, financing, and regulatory systems can consistently rely on. It needs to see where workers are deployed and where shortages persist, so that staffing can be planned against population need rather than inferred from incomplete or paper-based records. And it needs to pay workers reliably, and to track the education, credentialing, and supervision through which competence is established and renewed over time.
