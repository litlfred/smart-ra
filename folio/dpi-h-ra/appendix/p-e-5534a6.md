**Goal:** a health system that ensures the right number and skill mix of trained health and care personnel with the right skills are in the right place at the right time to meet population health needs, and a health workforce whose members — across both regulated professions and non-licensed or community cadres — are individually identifiable and verifiable (**Sub-goal 2.1**), equitably deployed and retained where they are needed (**Sub-goal 2.2**), paid reliably and on time in compliance with labour rights (**Sub-goal 2.3**), supported to maintain and extend their competencies across the course of their careers (**Sub-goal 2.4**), and backed by trustworthy registration and authorisation status that safeguards patient safety, public accountability, and worker privacy (**Sub-goal 2.5**).
