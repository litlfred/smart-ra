| **#** | **Business process supported** | **Functional requirement** | **Status** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Content Management** |   |   |   |
| **1** | Product master data management | **Product data management.** The Product Registry shall support the capture, storage, and publication of standardised product information for medicines, vaccines, medical devices, diagnostics, and health commodities, including regulatory status (where regulation applies, with explicit exempt, unregistered, and emergency-use categories) and the ability to store product images and supporting documentation. | **Required** |
| **2** | Product master data management · Procurement & contracting | **Multi-classification support.** The Product Registry shall support the simultaneous classification of products under multiple international and national classification schemes, including UNSPSC, GPC, and ATC, and the WHODrug data dictionary (for medicinal products), and shall allow additional classification schemes to be configured without modification to the core data model. | **Required** |
| **3** | Inventory management · Order management · Distribution | **Packaging hierarchy management.** The Product Registry shall support the definition and maintenance of packaging hierarchies for each product, capturing the validated GTIN (or national packaging identifier), unit of measure, and quantity conversion factor for each packaging level (unit, inner pack, case, pallet). | **Required** |
| **4** | Dispensing & point of care · Procurement | **Generic-to-trade-item mapping.** Where applicable to the product class, the Product Registry shall support the explicit mapping of generic product definitions to the specific branded trade items associated with them, including the maintenance of brand name, manufacturer name, and country of origin for each item record. The registry shall represent this mapping as an identity-grouping input to clinical substitution and procurement equivalence assessments — which additionally require regulatory, quality, formulation, strength, dosage form, route of administration, packaging, and policy verification — rather than as proof of clinical interchangeability or procurement suitability on its own. | **Required** |
| **5** | Product master data management · Regulatory compliance | **Product history, provenance, and versioning.** The Product Registry shall capture and retain the full history of changes made to product records, including attribute-level source provenance, the nature of the change, the date and time, and the verified submitter and approver who made and authorised it, supporting retrospective audit and impact analysis. | **Required** |
| **Identifier Management** |   |   |   |
| **6** | System integration · Order management | **Multi-scheme identifier validation and management.** The Product Registry shall record, validate, and manage product identifiers across multiple schemes — recording and validating externally assigned global identifiers (such as Global Trade Item Numbers (GTINs) issued by authorised organisations under GS1 rules, or ISO UDIs) rather than issuing GTINs itself, assigning nationally defined product codes only under the appropriate national authority and governance rules, and maintaining the mappings between schemes. | **Required** |
| **7** | System integration · Procurement | **Level-aware identifier cross-referencing.** The Product Registry shall support level-aware mappings between national product identifiers and standardised global identifiers (e.g., GTIN, UDI) that explicitly distinguish generic product concepts, specific trade items, and packaging hierarchy levels (unit, inner pack, case, pallet). Each mapping shall record the mapping type, purpose, equivalence scope, validity period, supporting evidence, and source provenance, and shall require human review and stewardship approval for ambiguous or non-one-to-one relationships. | **Required** |
| **Governance Workflow & Access Control** |   |   |   |
| **8** | Governance & data stewardship | **Access control, organisational verification, and submission trust.** The Product Registry shall enforce role- and attribute-based access control, restricting the ability to create, update, and deprecate product records or specific attribute domains to verified organisations, users, and integrated systems with delegated authority. For manufacturer submissions and automated source integrations, the registry shall support organisational and credential verification, data segregation, submission provenance capture, digital signatures where appropriate, and strict separation of submitter and approver roles, while providing read-only access to consuming systems. | **Required** |
| **9** | Governance & data stewardship | **Attribute- and product-class-specific approval workflows.** The Product Registry shall support configurable, attribute- and product-class-specific workflows for the review and approval of updates to product information, routing proposed changes to the designated approvers for each attribute domain (such as regulatory authorisation, clinical classification, logistics and packaging attributes, or core master-data stewardship), enforcing source-authority and precedence rules, supporting conflict escalation, and requiring independent approver sign-off before changes are published to consuming systems. | **Required** |
| **10** | Product master data management · Governance | **Activation, deactivation, and urgent regulatory status propagation.** The Product Registry shall support the activation, deactivation, suspension, withdrawal, deprecation, and retirement of product records, with immediate priority event notification to consuming systems when urgent regulatory actions (such as recalls, suspensions, or market withdrawals) or lifecycle status changes occur. | **Required** |
| **Data Onboarding & Integration** |   |   |   |
| **11** | Data onboarding & migration | **Bulk data onboarding and migration.** The Product Registry shall support bulk onboarding or an equivalent migration capability for product master data from external sources (including regulatory registers, national procurement and donor catalogues, manufacturer submissions, and structured data files), providing schema and business-rule validation, record matching, staging and dry-run preview, role-separated approval, transactional rollback, and detailed error reporting. | **Required** |
| **12** | Data onboarding · System integration | **Strategy-driven external source integration.** Conditional on a country-specific source strategy, the Product Registry shall support system-to-system or scheduled integration with external authoritative data sources — illustrated by national regulatory authority registers, WHO prequalification listings, national procurement and donor catalogues, medical-device registries, international product databases, manufacturer systems, the Global Data Synchronisation Network (GDSN), the WHODrug data dictionary, and national ISO IDMP implementations. Each integration shall be governed by a country source strategy defining source-authority assessment, attribute-level precedence, verification, provenance, licensing and availability constraints, level-aware mapping, reconciliation, and fallback mechanisms. | **Recommended** |
| **13** | System integration · Supply chain operations | **Downstream system integration.** The Product Registry shall expose machine-readable APIs enabling consuming systems — including forecasting, order management, warehouse, procurement, cold chain, clinical, and dispensing systems — to retrieve product data in a consistent and portable manner. | **Required** |
| **Cross-Cutting** |   |   |   |
| **14** | Governance & data stewardship · Regulatory compliance | **Audit logging.** The Product Registry shall maintain a comprehensive, tamper-evident audit log of all submissions, authoring actions, approval decisions, conflict resolutions, publication events, access control changes, and bulk onboarding or rollback operations. | **Required** |
| **15** | System integration · Supply chain operations | **Subscription and notification.** The Product Registry shall provide a mechanism for consuming systems to subscribe to changes in specific product records, regulatory statuses, or product categories and to receive timely notifications (including priority alerts for urgent recalls, suspensions, and withdrawals) when records are created, updated, deprecated, or retired. | **Recommended** |
| **16** | System integration · Supply chain operations | **Performance and availability.** The Product Registry shall be designed for high availability, given that supply chain and clinical systems at all levels of the health system depend on access to product master data for operational transactions. | **Required** |
