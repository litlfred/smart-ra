The Terminology Service enables consistent interpretation of disease concepts, pathogen identifiers, clinical findings, and other coded inputs to surveillance. ICD-11-coded diagnoses and relevant postcoordinated detail may be consumed directly where they satisfy the surveillance use case, without a mandatory intermediate terminology conversion. The PHSP shall separately govern the case definition, evidence and laboratory requirements, time window, classification of suspected or confirmed cases, and reporting rules. Terminology updates shall trigger impact assessment; they shall not silently change a case definition or retrospectively reinterpret historical cases. Original clinical and laboratory evidence, coding releases, and derivation rules shall remain traceable.
