**The Terminology Service** ensures that clinical data drawn from the LHR and other source systems carries consistent coded meaning when ingested by the HMIS. Aggregate indicators depend on the semantic consistency of the underlying data; without governed terminology, the same clinical concept recorded with different codes in different systems would be aggregated incorrectly or incompletely.

Where the source record is coded directly in ICD-11, the HMIS should reuse that representation and derive the required statistical output using the selected MMS release and applicable morbidity or mortality rules. It should retain traceability from the output to the original clinical statement, including any more detailed Foundation concept or postcoordinated expression. Where source data uses another terminology, the mapping and classification process should be identified separately, with unresolved cases and loss of detail visible. A single coded diagnosis is not, by itself, a complete case definition, episode definition, denominator, or reporting rule.
