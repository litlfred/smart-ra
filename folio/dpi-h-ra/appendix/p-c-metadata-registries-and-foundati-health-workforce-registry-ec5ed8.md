The WHO National Health Workforce Accounts (NHWA) handbook (Second Edition, 2023) defines 29 indicators that underpin health workforce monitoring, planning, and policy — covering stock, flow, education, migration, and expenditure. The national health workforce registry is a key data source for 12 of these indicators, notably those measuring stock and flow – critical to workforce planning and equitable distribution. The Health Workforce Registry, as defined in the DPI-H reference architecture, provides the foundational data infrastructure from which these indicators can be derived.
