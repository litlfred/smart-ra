The challenge is compounded by how clinical knowledge is typically embedded in health systems. Where digital tools exist, clinical guidance is frequently hard-coded into individual point-of-care applications rather than governed centrally as a shared resource. When a guideline is updated, every application that contains a hard-coded version must be independently updated — a process that is slow, expensive, and frequently incomplete. The consequence is a health system in which different facilities, programmes, and systems simultaneously operate on different versions of the same clinical guidance, with no mechanism for ensuring consistency or currency across the ecosystem.
