The Supplier and Contracts Management System is the Supplier Registry's primary operational peer. The registry provides the authoritative supplier identity data; the Contracts Management System manages the contractual relationships, performance monitoring, and dispute resolution that depend on that identity. Every supplier contract and every supplier performance record should reference the canonical supplier identifier from the registry. A supplier that appears in a contract management system without a corresponding record in the Supplier Registry represents a gap in data governance — analogous to a facility receiving supplies without a Health Facility Registry record or a patient being seen without a Client Registry identifier
