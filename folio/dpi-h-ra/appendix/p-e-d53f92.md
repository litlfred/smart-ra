Unlike the goals that follow, this one has no mapping of its own. It is the foundation the other six draw on. Each of those mappings traces back to the same shared components — the client, health facility, and health workforce registries, the terminology service, the product registry, and the lifelong health record — and the recurrence of those components across goal after goal is the "capture once, use many times" principle made visible. The composition of that foundation, the minimum a country puts in place first, and how it matures over time are set out in the REDDHI framework in Section 2.2; the spreadsheets in this appendix show that foundation being reused, goal by goal.
