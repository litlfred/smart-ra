Requirements are grouped by business process and draw on the OpenHIE Facility Registry specification (FRF-1 to FRF-14; FRWF-1), expert feedback, the WHO/USAID MFL Resource Package, the UNICEF Health Facility Registry Toolkit (2024), and WHO Geolocated Health Facilities Data initiative. Where requirements extend or elevate beyond OpenHIE, this is noted. Several OpenHIE requirements are adjusted: FRF-3 (minimum dataset) is elevated to Required given the WHO GHFD initiative's alignment; FRF-6 (standards-based APIs) is elevated to Required; and versioning is elevated to Required to reflect the expert consensus on its criticality for temporal data integrity.
