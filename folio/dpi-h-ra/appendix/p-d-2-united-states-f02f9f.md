The United States illustrates centralized knowledge governance with distributed consumption through the CDC immunization decision-support logic (**CDSi**), consumed by a diverse ecosystem of immunization systems and clinical applications[^99], and population-scale syndromic surveillance (**BioSense**) that draws on clinical data already generated at the point of care[^100]. Both illustrate the governance of shared capability across a highly decentralized system with multiple jurisdictional authorities.
