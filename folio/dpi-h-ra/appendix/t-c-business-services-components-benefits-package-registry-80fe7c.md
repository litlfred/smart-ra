| **ARCHITECTURE PLACEMENT** | The Benefits Package Registry sits at the business services layer of the DPI-H reference architecture as the authoritative publication capability for benefit package definitions, coverage parameters, and related machine-readable reference rules. It references and depends on core registries (Client, Health Facility, Health Workforce, Product) and the Terminology Service, while explicitly excluding operational scheme administration, beneficiary enrolment, person-specific eligibility determination, provider empanelment and contracting, negotiated tariff and fee-schedule management, prior authorisation, executable claims adjudication, and payment execution. Those operational processes sit in the functional application layer — most notably in (1) the Contract and Benefits Management System (CBMS), which manages payer-provider contracts, tariffs, pre-authorisation, claims adjudication, and payment instructions; and (2) the Beneficiary Registry, which records which individuals are enrolled in which schemes, reconciled against the shared Client Registry identity. The Benefits Package Registry is the shared reference publication that both depend on. |
