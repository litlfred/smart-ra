These requirements are consistent with and extend the OpenHIE Shared Health Record specification (SHRWF-1, SHRWF-2; SHRF-1 to SHRF-9). The DPI-H reference architecture adopts 'Lifelong Health Record' in preference to 'Shared Health Record' to reflect the longitudinal, federated, and person-centred scope of the capability. The DPI-H requirements extend OpenHIE in the following respects: consent management integration is made an explicit requirement; federated and distributed architecture support is introduced; individual access and portability is added; offline and low-connectivity operation is specified; and data quality and reconciliation is made explicit. Where OpenHIE marks requirements as Recommended, the DPI-H reference architecture has elevated several to Required given their centrality to the clinical safety and governance objectives of the LHR. All OpenHIE SHR requirements that apply are noted.
