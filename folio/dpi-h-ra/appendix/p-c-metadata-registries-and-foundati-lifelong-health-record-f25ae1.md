The Computable Decision Support Engine (CDSE) consumes person-level clinical data from the LHR to evaluate decision logic, generate recommendations, and surface alerts at the point of care. The CDSE does not maintain its own copy of clinical data; it queries the LHR in context and at the point of invocation. The quality and completeness of the LHR directly determines the reliability of the outputs the CDSE can produce. This dependency means that clinical decision support cannot be meaningfully improved in isolation from the data quality and coverage of the LHR.
