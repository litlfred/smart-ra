*Figure A.1 — Semantic governance capabilities, the roles that carry them out, and the supporting application components.*

**Automated structural validation versus expert review in Monitoring.** In the **Monitoring** category (and throughout release validation), governance rules must explicitly **distinguish automated structural validation from expert semantic, clinical, policy, safety, and implementation review**. **Automated validation** — such as schema and profile conformance checks, canonical URI and dependency integrity, value-set expansion verification, concept-map structural checks, and automated staleness or drift detection — is essential for verifying formal syntactic and structural constraints consistently at scale, but it cannot establish clinical appropriateness, semantic equivalence of translations or mappings, legal or ethical acceptability, patient safety, or real-world usability. Continuous SGI monitoring must therefore combine automated structural checks with periodic and event-triggered **expert semantic, clinical, classification/coding, policy, legal, patient-safety, and implementation review**, using field uptake telemetry and implementer feedback to detect semantic drift, unsafe coding patterns, or usability barriers in deployed systems.
