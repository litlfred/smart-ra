Within the supply chain domain, the Product Registry is consumed by every operational system – forecasting and planning systems use product identifiers to aggregate demand signals; order management and warehouse systems use validated GTINs, national codes, and packaging hierarchy data to match orders to receipts and manage stock; procurement and supplier contract systems reference product identifiers and regulatory status when creating and evaluating tenders; cold chain and transportation systems apply storage requirements and volumetric data held in the registry to plan and monitor product movement; and dispensing, formulary, and procurement systems use conditional generic-to-trade-item mappings as an identity-grouping input when evaluating candidate items against clinical substitution and procurement equivalence rules.
