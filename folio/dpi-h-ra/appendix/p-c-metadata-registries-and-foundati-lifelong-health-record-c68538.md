The Health Management Information System draws on data held in the LHR as a primary source for aggregate indicators and programme performance monitoring, deriving population-level health information from person-level clinical data rather than requiring separate reporting from health facilities. The Public Health Surveillance Platform draws on LHR data for notifiable disease reporting, case investigation, and epidemiological analysis, enabling surveillance functions to be grounded in clinical data already captured at the point of care. Both relationships reduce the need for parallel data collection and support the architecture's principle of capturing data once and using it many times. The LHR provides person-level clinical data that these systems aggregate and, where appropriate, de-identify for secondary use.
