**What the health system needs to be able to do:** the system needs to forecast demand and plan supply against budgets and programme priorities, and to see stock levels and product movement from source to point of use. It needs to identify products, locations, suppliers, and individual batches consistently, so that an item can be traced through ordering, storage, transport, and dispensing, and recalled or removed when needs arise. And it needs to connect these flows to the rest of the health system — to facilities, to financing, and to the people who ultimately receive the products.
