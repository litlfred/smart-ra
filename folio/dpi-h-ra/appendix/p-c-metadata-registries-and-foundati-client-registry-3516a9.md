***Notes:*** *(1) 'Biometric data' is listed as Optional rather than Recommended. Its use requires a formal privacy impact assessment, an explicit legal basis, and an equity analysis to confirm it does not create access barriers for populations with unreadable biometrics (elderly, manual workers, persons with disabilities). (2) Countries should not embed personally identifiable attributes — name, date of birth, location — within the HUID code itself. The HUID should be an opaque, non-intelligent identifier. (3) The minimum dataset above reflects the nationally held Client Registry record; facility-level systems may collect additional clinical demographic attributes that do not need to be federated to the national registry.*
