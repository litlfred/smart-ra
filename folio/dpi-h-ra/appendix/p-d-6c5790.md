**Note on the naming of systems.** The systems, tools and national programmes named in this appendix are cited descriptively, to illustrate approaches adopted by countries at a point in time and are drawn from publicly available sources. Naming reflects each country's own implementation choice. Their inclusion does not constitute endorsement, recommendation or an assessment of suitability by WHO or ITU for any particular context, and no ranking or comparative evaluation between named systems is intended or implied. The transferable observations offered under each example hold independently of any specific product.
