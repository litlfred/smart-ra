| **#** | **Business process supported** | **Functional requirement** | **Status** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Content Management** |   |   |   |
| **1** | Benefit package design & definition | **Benefit package authoring.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support the creation, update, publication, deprecation, and retirement of benefit package definitions, with full version control and the ability to manage concurrent active package versions across different schemes. | **Required** |
| **2** | Benefit package design & definition · Claims validation reference | **Benefit structure definition.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support the structured definition and publication of what each package covers, including services, procedures, diagnostics, medicines, devices, exclusions, co-payment and cost-sharing parameters, utilisation caps, annual benefit limits, and general conditions under which covered items may be accessed, as authoritative reference content consumed by operational contracting and claims systems. | **Required** |
| **3** | Eligibility criteria publication · Beneficiary enrolment support (FISF-01) | **Eligibility criteria and reference rule definition.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support the formal definition and publication of general population-level eligibility criteria for each benefit package — including demographic, socioeconomic, employment-status, disease-specific, and geographic criteria — expressed as structured, testable machine-readable rule artefacts that consuming operational systems (such as a Beneficiary Registry, CBMS, or a separate governed rules service) can evaluate at runtime without the registry itself executing individual eligibility determinations. | **Required** |
| **4** | Scheme governance & oversight · Coverage planning | **Multi-scheme support.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support the coexistence of benefit packages from different scheme types — government-funded entitlements, contributory insurance, community-based schemes, donor-financed programmes, disease-specific subsidies — under a unified registry structure, without prescribing a single financing model or institutional ownership arrangement. | **Required** |
| **5** | Scheme governance & oversight · Strategic purchasing | **Payer and scheme attribution.** The Benefits Package Registry shall capture the payer or funding source responsible for each benefit package, including the identity of the administering institution, the financing mechanism, and the contractual or regulatory basis under which the package is offered, supporting multi-payer and mixed public-private financing environments. | **Required** |
| **6** | Provider empanelment reference · Point-of-care coverage lookup | **Facility tier and provider category linkage.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support the association of benefit packages with the general facility types, care tiers, service levels, and practitioner qualification categories through which covered services may be accessed (referencing the Health Facility Registry and Health Workforce Registry), while keeping provider-specific empanelment, payer-provider contracts, and payment eligibility in operational Contract and Benefits Management Systems. | **Recommended** |
| **Access, Eligibility Support & Integration** |   |   |   |
| **7** | System integration & interoperability | **Machine-readable APIs.** The Benefits Package Registry shall expose machine-readable APIs enabling consuming systems to query published package definitions, coverage parameters, general eligibility criteria, testable reference rule artefacts, payer associations, and facility-tier linkages in a consistent, portable manner, aligned where applicable with design-time FHIR resources (`InsuranceProduct`, `InsurancePlan`) that operational systems link to person-specific `Coverage` resources. | **Required** |
| **8** | Provider eligibility inquiry (FISF-03) · Beneficiary eligibility inquiry (FISF-04) | **Reference data support for eligibility inquiries.** The Benefits Package Registry shall provide the structured benefit definitions and testable coverage rule artefacts required by operational eligibility services to answer provider-initiated and beneficiary-initiated eligibility inquiries, while person-specific enrolment lookup and runtime eligibility determination remain in the Beneficiary Registry, CBMS, or a separate governed rules service. | **Required** |
| **9** | Pre-authorisation (FISF-05) | **Reference data support for pre-authorisation.** The Benefits Package Registry shall publish the coverage definitions and prior-authorisation criteria needed by operational pre-authorisation workflows to determine whether a proposed service requires and qualifies for prior authorisation, without the registry itself executing individual pre-authorisation transactions or clinical determinations. | **Recommended** |
| **10** | Eligibility criteria publication · Cross-sector coordination | **External eligibility rule referencing.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support declarative references to eligibility conditions that depend on data held in external registries — including social protection databases, household income or poverty classification systems, and employment registries — without replicating or owning that external data, leaving runtime person-level verification to operational enrolment and eligibility systems under data-minimisation safeguards. | **Required** |
| **11** | Coverage gap analysis & planning · Transparency & accountability | **Cross-scheme coverage query.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support authorised queries across all registered benefit packages, enabling analysts and administrators to determine which schemes are active, what services are covered under each, which population groups are eligible, and where coverage gaps or benefit overlaps exist. | **Recommended** |
| **Governance Workflow & Access Control** |   |   |   |
| **12** | Scheme governance & oversight · Data stewardship | **Role-based access control.** The Benefits Package Registry shall enforce role-based access control, differentiating between users and systems authorised to author and publish package definitions, those authorised to manage eligibility criteria and payer associations, and those with read-only access for eligibility and claims validation purposes. | **Required** |
| **13** | Scheme governance & oversight · Benefit package design | **Governance workflow.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support configurable review and approval workflows for the creation and modification of benefit package definitions, requiring designated stewards — which may include Ministry of Health officials, scheme regulators, and payer representatives — to approve changes before publication. | **Required** |
| **14** | Transparency & accountability · Patient communication | **Stakeholder visibility and plain-language descriptions.** The Benefits Package Registry shall support configurable visibility levels for benefit package definitions — enabling public access for beneficiary-facing applications while restricting draft or internal administrative parameters — and shall support associating plain-language, multilingual, accessible benefit summaries with machine-readable definitions so consuming portals and assisted-access channels can communicate entitlements clearly to patients. | **Recommended** |
| **Cross-Cutting** |   |   |   |
| **15** | Claims validation · Governance & data stewardship | **Version management.** The Benefits Package Registry shall maintain full version history of all benefit package definitions, supporting retrospective lookup of prior package versions so that operational claims and eligibility systems can validate encounters against the package definition that was in effect at the time of service delivery. | **Required** |
| **16** | System integration · Claims management | **Subscription and notification.** The Benefits Package Registry shall provide a mechanism for consuming systems — including operational eligibility services, CBMS and claims systems, and beneficiary-facing portals — to subscribe to changes in specific packages or scheme categories and receive notifications when definitions or rule artefacts are updated, deprecated, or retired. | **Recommended** |
| **17** | Governance & data stewardship · Transparency & accountability | **Audit logging.** The Benefits Package Registry shall maintain a comprehensive audit log of all authoring actions, publication events, access control changes, and bulk import operations, supporting accountability for benefit definition governance. | **Required** |
| **18** | Reference publication availability · Operational system synchronisation | **Performance, caching, and availability.** The Benefits Package Registry shall be designed for high availability and support reliable caching and synchronisation of published package definitions and rule artefacts by consuming operational systems, so that high-volume runtime eligibility determination and claims adjudication in operational systems are not bottlenecked by registry lookups. | **Required** |
