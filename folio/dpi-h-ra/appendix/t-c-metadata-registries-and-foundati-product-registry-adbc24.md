| **ARCHITECTURE PLACEMENT** | The Product Registry is a foundational DPI-H component holding core product master data — standardised, governed, and cross-sectorally reusable. Depending on national institutional and legal arrangements, it may operate directly as the regulatory authority's product register, as a central national product master-data hub consolidating records from authoritative sources, or as a federated reference view linking separate authoritative registers (such as distinct medicines and medical-device market-authorisation registers) under explicit attribute-level precedence rules. It is architecturally distinct from the Product Catalogue Management Tool, which extends the registry with richer, operationally dynamic attributes specific to health supply chain and clinical use cases (pricing, therapeutic substitution rules, procurement equivalence criteria, clinical interactions, adverse event linkage, and programme-specific listing rules). The Product Registry earns its foundational status because the same core product identity and regulatory-recognition data is needed across health, social protection, customs, regulatory authorities, and any other government function that transacts in health commodities. Countries should establish the Product Registry foundation first; the Product Catalogue Management Tool builds on it. |
