The most architecturally significant dependency of the Health Facility Registry is on geospatial infrastructure — specifically a Common Geo-Registry (CGR); a cross-sectoral foundational component that sits outside the health sector and serves the whole of government \cite{healthgeolab-common-geo-registry}. A CGR would manage geographic objects — administrative units, settlements, boundaries, and their hierarchical relationships — with full temporal integrity: it would record not just current geographies but historical changes, enabling any system to query the state of a geography on any given date.
