The tools and technologies listed in Section D.1 are software components, servers and applications that may be used to implement or support the capabilities described in this guidance; their inclusion does not imply that any of them constitutes shared infrastructure or digital public infrastructure in its own right. Several are commonly deployed at facility or programme level rather than as national shared infrastructure, and whether a given capability functions as shared infrastructure is determined by how a country deploys and governs it, not by the tool itself.
