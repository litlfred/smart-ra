Beyond the health supply chain, the Supplier Registry is a potential shared resource with other government functions. Finance systems use supplier identity for payment and audit; customs and trade authorities use it for import and export compliance; regulatory bodies use it for market authorisation and quality assurance. Where a cross-sectoral foundational supplier registry exists, the health sector should reference it rather than duplicating it. Where it does not, the health sector's Supplier Registry should be designed and governed with cross-sectoral reuse in mind from the outset.
