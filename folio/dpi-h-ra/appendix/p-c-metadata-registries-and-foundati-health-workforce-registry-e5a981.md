**Note:** *Requirements align with the* *OpenHIE* *Health Worker Registry specification (HWWF-1 to HWWF-4; HWRF-1 to HWRF-12). The DPI-H reference architecture extends* *OpenHIE* *in five respects: (1) licensing and credential management are made explicit as a distinct requirement group; (2) NHWA reporting alignment is introduced as a new group; (3) the verification service is elevated to Required; (4) audit logging (HWRF-6) is elevated from Recommended to Required given patient safety implications; and (5) facility deployment and* *mCSD* *joint querying are made explicit requirements. Sources:* *OpenHIE* *HWR Specification (https://guides.ohie.org/arch-spec/openhie-component-specifications-1/openhie-health-worker-registry-hwr); WHO NHWA Second Edition (2023); WHO Minimum Dataset for Health Workers (2015); WHO Classifying Health Workers (2010).*
