The Benefits Package Registry is the national authoritative publication of health benefit package definitions offered within a country's health financing ecosystem. It holds the definitive reference description of what each package covers, who defines it, who finances it, and the general population-level criteria under which people may be eligible for it — spanning government-funded entitlements, social health protection schemes, community-based programmes, donor-supported benefits, disease-specific subsidies, private insurance schemes, and NGO-run schemes. Its defining purpose is to provide a shared, governed, and machine-readable reference publication (including testable rule artefacts where appropriate) against which any system in the health ecosystem can determine what a given benefit package contains, without duplicating that definition or turning the registry itself into an individual eligibility or claims adjudication engine.
