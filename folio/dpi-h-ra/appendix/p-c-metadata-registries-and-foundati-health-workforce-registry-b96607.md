The following table defines the minimum dataset for a health worker record in the Health Workforce Registry. It draws on the WHO Minimum Dataset for Health Workers (WHO, 2015), the WHO National Health Workforce Accounts handbook (WHO NHWA Second Edition, 2023), and the WHO health worker classification framework (WHO, 2010). Status reflects the minimum requirement for a record to support identity resolution, credential verification, and workforce analytics. Mandatory elements should be present for a record to be published as active.
