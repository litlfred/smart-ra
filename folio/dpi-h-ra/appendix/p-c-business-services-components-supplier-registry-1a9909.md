A governed Supplier Registry makes the consistent, verified supplier identity available as a service to all supply chain systems, reducing duplication of supplier verification effort, improving the reliability of regulatory compliance checking, enabling more effective response to supply disruptions, and supporting end-to-end traceability by anchoring product and transaction records to verified supplier identities. It also enables better procurement decisions: when lead times, geographic constraints, and minimum order requirements are governed centrally rather than maintained in individual procurement systems, demand planning and supplier sourcing become more reliable and more equitable across the network.
