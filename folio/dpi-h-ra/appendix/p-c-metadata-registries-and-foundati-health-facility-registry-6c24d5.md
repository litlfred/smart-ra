***Notes:*** *(1) The WHO GHFD initiative identifies four minimum data elements for any global health facility record: Name, Type, Location (geographic coordinates), and Unique ID. These four are the minimum for international comparability. All other elements in the signature domain are required for full national registry functionality. (2) Facility type classification should be governed through the Terminology Service, not as a free-text field, to ensure cross-system comparability. (3) Administrative hierarchy linkages should reference the canonical identifiers from the Common Geo-Registry (where available) or national administrative unit register, not locally assigned text names. (4) The service domain elements are marked Recommended; their completeness depends on country capacity and use case requirements.*
