**What the health system needs to be able to do:** the health system needs authoritative, uniquely identified reference data — who people are, who provides care, where, with what products, and under what terms — held once and made available to every application component that needs it, rather than re-collected and reconciled in each. It needs that data to be well governed, current, and trustworthy, so that the systems built upon it can rely on it without duplicating or re-verifying it.
