When a Product Registry is in place, systems that participate in the health supply chain draw from the same product master. For example, forecasting systems can aggregate demand across facilities using consistent identifiers, and warehouse systems across different tiers of the supply chain can match incoming deliveries to orders without manual reconciliation, even when the delivering party and receiving system use different internal identifiers — because both resolve to the same canonical product record. These enabling conditions for a supply chain that reliably gets the right product to the right place in the right condition, also produce efficiency gains.
