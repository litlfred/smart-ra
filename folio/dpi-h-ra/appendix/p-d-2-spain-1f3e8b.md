Spain implements a federated national record (**Historia Clínica Digital del SNS**) that shares key clinical documents across seventeen autonomous communities through a national interoperability node, without centralizing clinical data \cite{es-ministerio-sanidad}. A federated national health information system aggregates indicators from the same devolved structure, and a national repository of logical information models (**SOMSNS**) maintains shared definitions \cite{somsns}. Spain demonstrates how a national interoperability framework can enable cross-jurisdictional exchange while preserving regional autonomy over data governance.
