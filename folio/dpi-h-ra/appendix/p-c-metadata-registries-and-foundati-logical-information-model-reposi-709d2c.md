There is no direct equivalent of the LIMR in the OpenHIE architecture specification. Requirements draw on internationally recognised standards and frameworks including HL7 FHIR Clinical Reasoning and Terminology modules, openEHR formalism specifications, and the WHO SMART Guidelines L3 framework. Interaction models and service definitions for LIMR-equivalent capabilities remain an area where standards gaps exist and the reference architecture identifies this as a gap to be addressed through future standardisation work.
