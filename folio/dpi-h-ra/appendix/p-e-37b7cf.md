| **Sub-goal / Mapping row** | **Health-system & worker outcome** | **Shared DPI-H & DPI-F capabilities** | **Operational systems, governance & standards** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **2.1 High-quality workforce data for planning, deployment and shortage mapping** *(Right Place / Equitable deployment and distribution)* | Ensure the right number and skill mix of trained health and care personnel are equitably distributed and retained across subnational areas and levels of care to meet population health needs, tracking NHWA *licensed to practise*, *professionally active*, and *practising* stock and inflows/outflows across the health labour market. | **Health Workforce Registry**, **Health Facility Registry**, and **Terminology Service** linked via **IHE mCSD** care-services discovery patterns (*Organisation*, *Practitioner*, *PractitionerRole*, *Location/Facility*, *HealthcareService*); **Health Management Information System** (aggregated service/workload metrics); foundational geospatial and data-exchange DPI. | Operational **HRHIS/HRIS**, deployment, scheduling, and **workforce-planning / health labour market modelling applications** combining workforce FTE, contract type, and scope of practice with catchment population need, service demand, workload, and facility infrastructure; interoperability with **population censuses, labour force surveys (national statistical offices), and national pension/social-security schemes**; purpose-limited de-identified/aggregated planning views. |
| **2.2 Reliable and timely remuneration and compensation analytics** *(Timely Pay)* | Pay health and care workers accurately, reliably, and on time in compliance with labour rights, eliminate ghost workers and payroll leakage, and **inform reporting on NHWA indicators on expenditure for compensation, entry-level wages and salaries, and pay gaps**. | **Health Workforce Registry** (verified workforce identity, active registration/authorisation, and primary/secondary facility affiliation references); **Health Facility Registry**; foundational **digital identity** and **payment DPI** disbursement rails. | Operational **payroll, HRIS, contract-management, time-and-attendance, payment-authorisation, and public financial management (PFM) systems** (which govern employment validity, salary and allowance calculation, deductions, extended leaves, administrative actions, and budget availability outside the shared registry); aggregated payroll feeds for NHWA remuneration and pay-gap indicators. |
| **2.3 Identity, verifiable qualifications, registration, clinical privileging, and cross-border mobility** *(Proven Skills)* | Establish governed identity, educational qualifications, and regulatory registration or alternative authorisation for both statutory licensed professionals and non-licensed/community cadres (CHWs, multiskilled workers, provisional staff, trainees, volunteers), separating registration from current clinical competence, task-specific authorisation, facility privileging, fitness to practise, and scope of practice. | **Health Workforce Registry**, **Terminology Service** (governing national occupation, role, specialty, credential, and scope-of-practice vocabularies mapped to **ISCO-08**), and foundational **identity, trust, and verifiable-credential DPI**. | **Health practitioner regulators** (licensing councils/boards), programme/employer authorisation workflows, facility **clinical privileging, supervision, and competence-assessment systems**; bilateral or regional credential-verification mechanisms where established by law, alongside foreign-trained stock and mobility monitoring aligned with the **WHO Global Code of Practice on the International Recruitment of Health Personnel** (which establishes ethical recruitment principles, workforce sustainability, and data exchange/reporting rather than mandating a specific digital cross-border verification protocol). |
| **2.4 Continuous competency and career development** *(Continuous Growth)* | Support health and care workers to maintain and extend their competencies, specialisations, and scopes of practice across their careers through accredited pre-service education, continuing professional development (CPD), and clinical supervision. | **Health Workforce Registry** (recording authoritative qualification references, specialist credentials, and high-level CPD compliance/licence-renewal status); **Terminology Service** hosting the **ISCED taxonomy** (International Standard Classification of Education) for educational qualifications alongside national credential and specialty code sets. | Operational **learning-management systems (LMS), pre-service education and training-tracking platforms, educational accreditation bodies, and clinical supervision systems** (which manage detailed learning activities, course completions, competency assessments, and training records outside the shared registry). |
| **2.5 Safety, accountability, and privacy-preserving workforce data governance** *(Cross-cutting trust & data protection)* | Enable patients, facilities, payers, and regulators to trust that a practitioner's registration, licence, or authorisation status is current — preventing payroll fraud and unsafe or uncredentialed care — while protecting worker privacy and labour rights. | **Health Workforce Registry** standards-based query APIs with role- and purpose-based access control, audit logging, and consent/disclosure controls; foundational **trust, authentication (SSO), and audit DPI**. | Compliance with national **data confidentiality, privacy, and data-protection laws**; **purpose-specific datasets** (restricting access to residential address, nationality, disciplinary records, salary, and HR admin notes); **worker transparency** into data access and formal procedures for **record correction and appeal**. |

**Normative references informing the Goal 2 mapping:** (1) World Health Organization (2023), *National Health Workforce Accounts: A Handbook*, Second Edition; (2) World Health Organization (2010), *Classifying Health Workers: Mapping Occupations to the International Standard Classification* (ISCO-08), together with the UNESCO *International Standard Classification of Education (ISCED)* taxonomy for education and qualifications; (3) World Health Organization (2015), *Human Resources for Health Information System: Minimum Data Set for Health Workforce Registry* (with updated WHO HRHIS guidance in preparation); and (4) World Health Organization (2010), *WHO Global Code of Practice on the International Recruitment of Health Personnel*.
