The Supplier Registry is the authoritative master data source for identity, classification, and regulatory status of suppliers engaged in the health supply chain — including manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, third-party logistics providers, importers, donor organisations, and NGO supply partners. It maintains a standardised, uniquely identified record for each supplier, providing the common reference point against which procurement, order management, warehouse, transport, and contract management systems can consistently identify, verify, and classify the organisations from which health products are sourced and through which they are distributed.
