The governance dimension is equally important. International terminology standards — SNOMED CT/GPS, ICD-11, LOINC — are updated regularly. National extensions to those standards, and national value sets built on them, require expert clinical and informatics review before changes are adopted. Without governance infrastructure, changes to international standards propagate unevenly into national implementations, creating a patchwork of systems running different versions of the same terminology and unable to reliably compare data across the versions. The TS is the institutional mechanism through which this change is managed, reviewed, versioned and disseminated in a controlled and traceable way.
