The Product Registry and the Product Catalogue Management Tool address different problems in the product data landscape. The registry provides the stable, cross-sectoral identity and regulatory-recognition layer — whether operated directly as the regulatory source, as a national master-data hub, or as a federated reference view linking authoritative regulatory and domain registers — governed by attribute-level source precedence, preserved provenance, defined regulatory update service levels (including urgent propagation of recalls, suspensions, and withdrawals), and mandatory regulatory status where regulation applies (including explicit exempt, unregistered, or emergency-use categories). The Product Catalogue Management Tool provides the enriched, operationally specific information — such as formulary status, clinical substitution rules, and procurement equivalence and pricing — that health system actors need to use, procure, and prescribe products appropriately. Both are necessary; neither is a substitute for the other.
