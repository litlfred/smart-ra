The **Computable Decision Support Engine** draws on aggregate performance data and population-level indicators from the HMIS to inform the updating and validation of clinical rules and guidelines. Population-level patterns detected through the HMIS — such as shifts in disease burden, treatment outcomes, or screening coverage — can trigger the review and refinement of decision support logic. The relationship is primarily unidirectional, with the HMIS informing knowledge governance processes that the CDSE operationalises at the point of care.
