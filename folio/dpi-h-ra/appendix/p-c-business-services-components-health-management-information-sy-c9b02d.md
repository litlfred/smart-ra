Analytics capabilities are integral to the HMIS rather than constituting a separate component. The distinction between data collection and aggregation on one hand, and analysis and visualisation on the other, is a functional distinction within the HMIS — both are capabilities of the same component, not separate systems. Both serve the same purpose — turning health data into health information — and both depend on the same foundational data infrastructure. The DPI-H reference architecture does not prescribe a single implementation model; the HMIS may be implemented as a unified platform or as a set of interoperable services, provided the functional outcomes described here are achieved.
