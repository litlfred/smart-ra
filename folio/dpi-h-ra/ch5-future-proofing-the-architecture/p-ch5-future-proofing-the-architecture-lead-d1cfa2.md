Artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies are reshaping expectations of what digital health systems can achieve. Machine learning is being applied to diagnostic and decision support, risk prediction, image interpretation, the detection and forecasting of outbreaks, operational planning, and resource allocation. Large language and multi-modal models are changing how clinical knowledge is summarised, retrieved, and acted upon. Beyond these current applications, advances in AI agents, remote monitoring, wearable sensors, genomics, edge computing, and verifiable digital credentials each carry implications for how health data is generated, exchanged, and used. The pace of this change is unlikely to slow.
