The definition of digital public infrastructure is itself evolving, and it is plausible that capabilities now treated as supporting technologies, including aspects of AI, may in time be recognised as reusable shared components in their own right. For the present, the architecture treats AI as a cross-cutting consideration rather than a defined component or a single platform, reflecting both how quickly the field is moving and the fact that analytical needs differ across health goals. The architecture remains open to revisiting that position as the technology and the surrounding consensus settle.
