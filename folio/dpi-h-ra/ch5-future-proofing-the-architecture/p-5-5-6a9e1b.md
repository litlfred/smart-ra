The reference architecture is accordingly designed to remain open and adaptable. Flexibility at the interfaces between components, and in how those components are implemented, allows emerging technologies — including those not yet foreseeable — to be incorporated and scaled as they prove their worth, without the foundations having to be rebuilt. The foundations themselves, however, should not be regarded as static; they require ongoing stewardship, maintenance, and incremental evolution to sustain data quality, respond to changing needs, and absorb new technologies as they emerge.
