Artificial intelligence is understood here broadly, as machine-based systems that, for a given set of objectives, infer from the data they receive how to generate outputs such as predictions, recommendations, classifications, or decisions. This spans rule-based systems, statistical and machine learning models, and the large language and multi-modal models whose rapid public adoption from the early 2020s brought AI to the centre of health-sector attention. Several jurisdictions are now establishing legal definitions and governance frameworks for these systems \cite{eu-2024-1689-ai-act}, and WHO has issued guidance on the ethics and governance of AI for health \cite{who-2021-ai-ethics-governance} and, more recently, on large multi-modal models specifically \cite{9789240084759-eng}.
