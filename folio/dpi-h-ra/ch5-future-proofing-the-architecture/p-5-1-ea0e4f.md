Emerging technologies do not substitute for the foundational work of building trusted registries, governed terminology, structured information models, and reliable [interoperability](../folio-assistant-core/glossary/instance/dpi-h-ra/#dpi-h-ra--dpi-h-ra--interoperability). More often they depend on that work and integrate with it. Artificial intelligence, in particular, operates on top of existing digital infrastructure; where that infrastructure is weak, fragmented, or poorly governed, AI is less likely to perform as intended, with consequences for quality and patient safety.
