The first goal is **building trusted data foundations**. The core principle is that health data should be "*captured once and used many times*”. Countries currently invest heavily in reporting systems where the same data is re-entered at multiple levels of the system, leading to high burden, low quality, and limited comparability. The architecture defines the shared infrastructure that enables a single, high-quality data capture to support clinical care, facility management, population analytics including CRVS and mortality, public health monitoring, and domestic AI development simultaneously.
