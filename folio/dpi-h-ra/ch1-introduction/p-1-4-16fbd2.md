The design and implementation of Digital Public Infrastructure for Health is guided by a set of principles that reflect the technical requirements of shared digital infrastructure and the responsibilities inherent in managing health data at population scale. At its foundation, DPI-H promotes the use of open standards and interoperable design, enabling systems to exchange data reliably and evolve without vendor lock-in. The principles below should inform every aspect of how DPI-H is conceived, built, governed, and sustained.
