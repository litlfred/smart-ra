According to the World Bank Group \cite{clark-2025-dpi-and-development}, DPI is an approach to digitalization focused on creating “foundational, digital building blocks designed for the public benefit.” They comprise a variety of digital software, platforms, APIs, and services, along with their related legal and regulatory frameworks, standards, policies, and processes.<sup>7</sup> Critically, by providing essential digital functions at society scale that can be reused across sectors, DPIs enable public and private service providers to build on these systems, innovate, and roll out new services more quickly and efficiently.
