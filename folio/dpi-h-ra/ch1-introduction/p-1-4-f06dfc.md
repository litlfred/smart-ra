These principles establish the foundational character of DPI-H as shared digital infrastructure. The reference architecture that gives effect to these principles is further shaped by a set of architectural design principles — including equity and inclusion across geographic, connectivity, institutional, linguistic, literacy, and functional-ability constraints, with assisted and non-digital routes of equivalent standing — that govern how DPI-H is designed, structured, and applied in practice, addressed in the Architecture Principles sub-chapter of the Reference Architecture Framework (Section 2.3).
