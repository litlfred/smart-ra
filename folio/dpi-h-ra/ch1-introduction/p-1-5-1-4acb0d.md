The fourth goal is **establishing** **trusted personal health records** with every person having a right to their own health record. The architecture supports individuals having access to secure, portable, and verifiable records of their health encounters, vaccinations, diagnoses, and treatments — records that follow them across providers and that they control through meaningful, accessible consent mechanisms, including assisted, delegated, and non-digital pathways of equivalent standing so that exercising rights over one's record never depends exclusively on individual smartphone ownership, fluency in an official language, or personal digital literacy.
