DPI-H does not eliminate the need for programme-specific and point-of-service applications. It provides the foundation upon which they can be built more efficiently, connected more reliably, and sustained more affordably — making every subsequent investment in the health system more productive than it would otherwise be. Because capabilities that depend on individual digital activation tend to be exercised first by those with higher income, education, connectivity, and digital confidence, the primary value case for DPI-H should rest on the systemic capabilities and continuity it creates for every person as a reference point regardless of whether they personally use a digital channel, while pairing any self-service capability with assisted and non-digital routes of equivalent standing.
