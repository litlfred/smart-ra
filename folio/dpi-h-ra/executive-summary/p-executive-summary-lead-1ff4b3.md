The guidance also introduces the concept of Resilient Essential Data and Digital Health Infrastructure (REDDHI) — the state that countries are working towards through their digital health investments — and describes the minimum set of shared capabilities and [application components](../folio-assistant-core/glossary/instance/dpi-h-ra/#dpi-h-ra--dpi-h-ra--application-component) that form the foundation of REDDHI. This provides countries with a practical entry point for their DPI-H journey, whatever their current context.
