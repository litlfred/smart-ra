Beyond the two layers, there are certain capabilities that are properties of DPI-H as a whole rather than features of any single component, layer, or business service. **Interoperability** is not a component to be built once but a property that should pervade the architecture through shared standards, common data models, and defined exchange patterns. **Governance** is cross-cutting in the same way: it applies to every component and capability the architecture describes and is treated as an architecture design principle below and elaborated on in the governance sub-chapter.
