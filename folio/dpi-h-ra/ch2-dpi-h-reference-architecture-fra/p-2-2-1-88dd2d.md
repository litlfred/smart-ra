REDDHI — Resilient Essential Data and Digital Health Infrastructure — describes the direction of travel that addresses this fragmentation: a governed, interoperable digital health infrastructure built on open standards and trusted, secure, quality data, within which local and regional innovation can flourish, investments can be mobilised and absorbed at scale, and the health system can sustain itself technically and financially over time. It is not a fixed destination to be reached and signed off, and it is not a set of systems to procure. It is a condition of self-sustaining national digital health maturity that continues to evolve as needs and technology change — one in which the foundational capabilities every health programme depends upon are shared, interoperable, and governed in the public interest rather than duplicated in silos.
