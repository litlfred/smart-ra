*Table 2.4: Health financing capabilities and the DPI-H components that supports them*

| **Financing capability** | **Supported** **by** |
| --- | --- |
| **Identifying and confirming who is covered** | Client Registry for canonical person identity (with foundational digital identity where available), consumed by operational beneficiary and enrolment systems that record scheme membership and entitlement status |
| **Empanelling, accrediting, and credentialing providers** | Health Facility Registry and Health Workforce Registry for authoritative facility licensing, service capability, and practitioner credential verification, referenced by operational provider empanelment and contract-management systems |
| **Defining covered services, schemes, and entitlement rules** | Benefits Package Registry for authoritative benefit definitions and machine-readable reference rules, consumed by operational eligibility, pre-authorisation, and claims systems |
| **Pricing and reimbursing accurately** | Product Registry and Terminology Services for standardised product and clinical coding, combined in operational financing systems with tariffs, fee schedules, payer-provider contracts, clinical coding rules, and claims adjudication logic |
| **Moving funds and reconciling payments** | Foundational payments DPI for payment initiation and settlement, instructed by operational claims, scheme-administration, payroll, and public financial management systems after financial-control and authorisation checks |
| **Exchanging financial and clinical data for payment** | Shared exchange standards (interoperability) connecting point-of-care, registry, social-protection, and scheme-administration systems under data-minimisation safeguards |
