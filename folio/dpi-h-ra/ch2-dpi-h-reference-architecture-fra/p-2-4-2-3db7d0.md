These shared components do not, by themselves, run a financing system. The work is carried out by the operational and functional applications that consume them — claims engines, scheme administration platforms, government financial management systems — which draw on the shared infrastructure to enrol people, adjudicate claims, and pay providers. These applications sit outside DPI-H: the architecture enables them rather than defining them, and the shared components reduce the duplication and fragmentation they would otherwise carry. This is the pattern the mapping is designed to surface, and it holds across all seven health goals.
