Countries should ensure that architectural decisions reflect and reinforce clear governance frameworks at every level — from the stewardship of individual DPI-H components to the oversight of the broader health digital ecosystem— with defined accountability structures, clear ownership of shared infrastructure, and arrangements proportionate to the sensitivity and criticality of the capabilities they oversee. Governance is a foundational architectural activity that precedes and shapes technical decisions rather than a project-level concern addressed after implementation, and it should not be assumed to be inherited from the frameworks or reference architectures a country adopts.
