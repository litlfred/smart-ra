A health system that shares data across many systems and institutions can only do so on the basis of trust: participants exchange information when they can be confident that the systems they exchange it with are what they claim to be, that the data is genuine and unaltered, and that sensitive information will be protected. Trust is a property the architecture has to be designed to establish and sustain across every component and exchange, and it underpins the trusted, secure data on which every other capability depends.
