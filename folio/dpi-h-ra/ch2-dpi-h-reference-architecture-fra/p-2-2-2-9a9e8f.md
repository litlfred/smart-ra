REDDHI is, in this sense, both a practical direction and a strategic commitment. As a practical direction it describes what countries work to put in place to move beyond fragmentation. As a strategic commitment it describes how — through governance, open standards, and shared infrastructure — countries can position themselves to mobilise, manage, and absorb large-scale investment in ways that build lasting capability rather than new dependencies. The reference architecture that follows sets out the components, relationships, and design decisions through which countries can pursue this continuously evolving goal.
