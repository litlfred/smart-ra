Adoption is thereby quantifiable: **coverage** is the proportion of reference elements realized by the country architecture, while the conformance of those covered elements is tested through their inherited criteria. This holds whether the country *adopts* the reference elements directly, *adapts* them (realization with documented modification), or *maps* its own elements onto them. Realization is the strongest mapping — it asserts that the country element fulfils the reference element's intent and so inherits its criteria; looser correspondences may use an Association or Specialization, but only realization carries that inheritance.
