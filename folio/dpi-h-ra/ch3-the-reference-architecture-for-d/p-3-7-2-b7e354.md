A **transaction** — a fully specified machine-to-machine protocol exchange, with defined trigger events, message semantics, and expected actions — is **one kind** of Application Interaction: its precise, specifiable subset. The architecture works at the broader level of interaction, because the same service is often reached by interactions that are not machine-to-machine transactions (see *Multiple access patterns to a single service*) and by exchanges not yet specified to transaction level. Where an interaction *is* specified as a transaction, that specification may be supplied by a framework such as IHE rather than restated here (see *Alignment with IHE profiles*).
