Each actor–service association additionally carries a support qualifier: the service may be **mandatory** or **optional** for that actor. A mandatory service is supported by any system claiming the actor; an optional service may be supported or not, and where it is, behaves as specified. This is independent of the offered/required *direction*, and corresponds to the *required*/*optional* (R/O) designation an IHE profile gives each transaction. ArchiMate has no native qualifier for it, so it is recorded as a **Property** on the serving relationship, or — where normative and testable — as a motivation-layer **Constraint**. (Note the two senses of "required": a *required service* is one the actor depends on; a *mandatory* service is one whose support is obligatory. This document reserves **offered/required** for the direction and **mandatory/optional** for support.)
