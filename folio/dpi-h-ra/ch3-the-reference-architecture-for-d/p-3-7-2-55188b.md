1. **The unit of conformance is the Application Component** (the system realising the actor), not the Application Interaction.
1. **Conformance is role-directional.** A system may conform as the *responder* without conforming as the *initiator*, and vice versa, because these are distinct services.
1. **Testing exercises one side of an interaction at a time.** The component under test plays one actor role; a **simulator** — itself an Application Component realising the *opposite* actor — plays the other side. The test verifies that the system-under-test's services behave as specified.
1. **The support qualifier determines what is tested.** Every *mandatory* service is tested; an *optional* service is tested only where the system declares support for it.
