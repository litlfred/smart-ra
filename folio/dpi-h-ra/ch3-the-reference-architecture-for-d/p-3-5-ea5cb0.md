This section introduces the application components that make up the digital public infrastructure for health: the shared, reusable digital resources that a country establishes once and that many programmes and points of care draw on. It describes what distinguishes such a component from the functional applications that sit on top of it, the consistent structure used to describe each one, and how the components are organised across the layers of the reference architecture. Full descriptions of every component — with their attributes, requirements, and governance considerations — are provided in Appendix C.
