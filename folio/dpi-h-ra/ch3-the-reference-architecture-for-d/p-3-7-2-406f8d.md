An **actor** is an abstract information-processing role — for example a *Document Source* or a *Patient Demographics Consumer*. It names a set of responsibilities, independently of any product that fulfils them. An actor is modelled as an **Application Component**. A concrete system — a vendor product, a national service — is also an Application Component, bound to the actor by a **realisation relationship**: *the system realises the actor*. One system may realise several actors; one actor may be realised by many systems.
