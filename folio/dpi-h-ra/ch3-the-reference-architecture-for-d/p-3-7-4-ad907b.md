These distinctions can also be expressed as measures, so that progress in testing is quantifiable rather than impressionistic. Three ratios are useful: *requirements coverage* — the requirements tested and confirmed satisfied, against the total stated for a component; *standards conformance* — the applicable standards for which conformance has been demonstrated, against the total identified; and *capability completeness* — the expected capabilities delivered and verified, against the total the architecture expects. Each counts confirmed results against the full set of expectations, and together they complement the realisation-based coverage measure described below.
