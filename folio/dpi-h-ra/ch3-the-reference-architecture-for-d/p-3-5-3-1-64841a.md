The Logical Information Model Repository (LIMR) and the Terminology Service (TS) are distinct but tightly coupled components necessary for semantic governance. Both address different dimensions of the same underlying problem: how to ensure that health data means the same thing wherever it is created, exchanged, or used. Whilst they are interdependent, the boundary is not always intuitive; and whilst one controls syntax (the structure and hierarchy of the language), the other controls semantics (meaning).
