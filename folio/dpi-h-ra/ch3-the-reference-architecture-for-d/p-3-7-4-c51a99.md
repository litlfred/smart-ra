This is an instance of a more general property. Because the elements and their relationships are **structured and machine-readable**, an architecture expressed in this way can be *assessed*, not merely described. When a country adapts the reference architecture, or adopts only selected parts of it, the model makes gaps explicit and checkable — for example a capability with no component realising it, an actor whose required service nothing provides, or an interaction with no conforming implementation. Completeness and consistency thereby become properties that can be checked against the model itself, informing decisions about what to adopt, extend, or build.
