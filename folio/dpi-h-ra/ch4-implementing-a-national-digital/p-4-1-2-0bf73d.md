The outputs of this assessment form the foundation of a national digital health architecture roadmap: a living document that maps the current state, the target direction, the gap between them, and the sequenced investments required to close it. The roadmap is strengthened where it also sets out a benefits case and a simple logic model — connecting each investment to the capabilities it enables and the health outcomes it is expected to support — and where it draws on recognised digital-readiness and capability assessment frameworks, to establish a baseline against which progress can later be measured.
