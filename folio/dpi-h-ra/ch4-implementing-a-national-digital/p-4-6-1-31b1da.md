For Ministries of Finance, the most persuasive case is grounded in outcomes they already track: improved budget execution, reduced leakage, timely provider payments, traceable health expenditure, and the efficiency gains that follow from replacing fragmented administrative systems with shared infrastructure. Framed this way, DPI-H reads as capital investment in health system infrastructure rather than a technology modernisation project — and that reframing is what justifies financing it as infrastructure: through multi-year, recurrent budget lines and domestic cost-recovery mechanisms, rather than the project-based donor cycles that treat digital infrastructure as a passing line item.<sup>42</sup> Ministries of Finance are best engaged not as a final approval step but as strategic partners in the long-term financing of shared services.
