For the core registries, minimum data models should address at least the following: for the client registry, the minimum demographic and identity attributes required to uniquely identify a person across health system interactions; for the facility registry, the minimum attributes required to describe a health service delivery point, including its location, service capabilities, and operational status; for the health workforce registry, the minimum attributes required to describe a licensed health worker, including their credentials, specialisation, and deployment; and for the product registry, the minimum attributes required to describe a regulated health product, including its identity, classification, and regulatory status. For terminology services and the logical information model repository, minimum profiles should address the metadata requirements for versioning, validity periods, and content integrity described in the semantic governance infrastructure section.
