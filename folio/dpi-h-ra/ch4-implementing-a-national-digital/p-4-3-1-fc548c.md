For the core registries, minimum data models should address at least the following: for the client registry, the minimum demographic and identity attributes required to uniquely identify a person across health system interactions — supporting alternative and assisted identification pathways where a foundational national identifier is absent, and capturing preferred language and communication or accessibility support needs without requiring unnecessary sensitive data that could expose vulnerable populations; for the facility registry, the minimum attributes required to describe a health service delivery point, including its location, service capabilities, accessibility features, connectivity profile, and operational status; for the health workforce registry, the minimum attributes required to describe a licensed or authorised health worker, including their credentials, specialisation, language capabilities, and deployment; and for the product registry, the minimum attributes required to describe a regulated health product, including its identity, classification, and regulatory status. For terminology services and the logical information model repository, minimum profiles should address the metadata requirements for versioning, validity periods, multilingual designations and cross-language mappings (so that history captured in a patient's language is faithfully represented in the working language and coded terminology of the health system), provenance for assisted or paper-bridged data capture, and content integrity described in the semantic governance infrastructure section.
