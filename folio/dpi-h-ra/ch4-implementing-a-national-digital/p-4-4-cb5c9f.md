Three of those uses connect directly to earlier sections. Conformance certification against published profiles can be made a condition of procurement (section 4.3), so that each system entering the ecosystem is verified against the role it claims. Coverage — the proportion of reference elements a national architecture realises, as defined in section 3.7 — gives a simple, repeatable measure of how much of the target architecture is in place. And the same mapping that establishes coverage surfaces gaps, which feed directly into the roadmap's gap analysis (section 4.1).
