**What do we already have?** Inventory all existing health information systems, registries, and digital tools, including those funded by development partners and individual programmes. For each, assess whether it performs a function that aligns with a REDDHI component, whether it is based on open standards, what population or geography it covers, and who owns and operates it.

Alongside the system inventory, inventory the clinical statements, forms, datasets, classifications, terminology releases, reporting obligations, and exchanges already in use. Trace the priority data flows from capture through clinical use to reporting, identifying who records or transforms the information, which rules apply, and where detail is lost or collected again. Evaluate candidate standards against the actual use cases, coverage, implementation maturity, maintained tooling, governance, conformance evidence, and total lifecycle cost. Record the reasons for each selection and any identified gaps.
