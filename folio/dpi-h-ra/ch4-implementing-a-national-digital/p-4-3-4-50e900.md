**General APIs that foundational DPI should enable for health sector use.** These include identity verification APIs that allow the client registry to validate a person’s identity against the national identity system and retrieve a verified national identifier; civil registration APIs that allow birth and death events to trigger updates in the client registry and health workforce registry; payment gateway APIs that allow health financing systems to initiate, verify, and reconcile payments through the national payments infrastructure; and data exchange APIs that allow health information to be shared securely through the national data exchange backbone where one exists.
