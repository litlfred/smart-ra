- **Component coverage** — which REDDHI components are in place, at what level of functionality, and with what geographic, linguistic, and population reach, including rural, remote, and offline-operating facilities and the availability of assisted and non-digital pathways of equivalent standing (the realisation-based coverage measure of section 3.7, applied over time).
- **Standards conformance** — the extent to which deployed components and existing systems conform to open technical, accessibility, multilingual, and offline-resilience standards, expose standard APIs, and use governed terminology and data models.
- **Governance maturity** — how developed the institutional, legal, participatory, and process foundations are that keep shared infrastructure maintained, governed, assessed for distributional equity impacts, and sustainably evolved.
- **Outcome linkage** — whether there is evidence that shared digital infrastructure is contributing to improved health outcomes, reduced administrative burden for frontline workers, and more efficient and equitable resource allocation across population groups, including those experiencing overlapping disadvantages, framed where possible against the explicit aims a country has adopted for its health system.
