**Health-sector-specific APIs that foundational DPI authorities could** **synchronise** **with.** These include the client registry’s patient matching and identity resolution APIs, which foundational identity authorities may need to consume when resolving health-related identity queries; the facility registry’s location and service capability APIs, which geographic information systems and emergency response services may consume; the health workforce registry’s credentialing verification APIs, which payment systems use to confirm provider eligibility before releasing funds; and the terminology services APIs, which any system exchanging coded health data — including those outside the health sector — needs to access to ensure consistent semantic interpretation.
