Describe HMIS scope as a multi-source aggregate-data platform
requirements
- Do not present the LHR as the primary source of aggregates - Structure the minimum dataset around aggregate observations and indicator metadata - Describe HMIS and PHSP as distinct capability domains that may share a platform - Distinguish aggregate-data receipt, indicator calculation, analytics and publication responsibilities - Reword to denominators for service-utilisation indicators and point to census and population projections for population denominators - Allow direct aggregate, event, tracker, paper/SMS and automated contributions with provenance and progressive automation - Limit the DPI-H component to reusable aggregate data, indicator, validation and publication services - State when data is managed as routine aggregate reporting, line-listed surveillance or case records
