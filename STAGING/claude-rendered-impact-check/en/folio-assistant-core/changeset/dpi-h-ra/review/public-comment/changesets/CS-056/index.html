Present terminology governance as federated with international SDOs
requirements
- Define the TS as a governed capability that may host, federate, mirror or provide access to sources - Describe the relationship between national SGI bodies and international SDOs (SNOMED International, Regenstrief, WHO-FIC, HL7) - State that TS and LIMR are core enablers but do not alone guarantee semantic interoperability - Decide whether and how to reference SNOMED International's offer and the NRC model - Represent sources beyond the global-national-facility hierarchy (regional, programme, professional, institutional) with governed publication and feedback - Consider raising federation/synchronisation with ICD-11, SNOMED CT and LOINC to Required and describe it - Preserve WHO and other publishers' authority over their identifiers - Address receiving, localising and distributing international releases on their schedules
