Define the LHR as governed longitudinal access, not a store
requirements
- Justify the LHR's place in the foundational layer and distinguish it visually from registries - Add a decision table comparing centralised, federated and hybrid models - Define the LHR at governance level (authoritative longitudinal view) and at implementation level (assembled from source systems, consolidated or federated) - Explain the choice of 'Lifelong Health Record' over 'Shared Health Record' - Define it as longitudinal access via consolidated, federated or hybrid means, not one national store - State that federation increases dependence on a governed information model - Replace 'maintains' with wording that does not imply a persisted national record - Add a comparison of LHR, SHR, EHR and patient summary
