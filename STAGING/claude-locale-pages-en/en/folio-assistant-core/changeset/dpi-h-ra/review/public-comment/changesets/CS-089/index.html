Base legacy disposition and transition on structured assessment
requirements
- Make integration conditional on assessment of safety, data quality, security, supportability and cost, not age alone - Include contracts, data, workforce and paper or hybrid workflows in the legacy baseline - Require transition planning well before contract end (migration, testing, parallel running, cutover) - Qualify the position against blank-slate approaches - Note that standards-based systems can still form silos and require shared governance, stewardship and funding coordination - Add these dimensions to legacy assessment with a documented disposition per system - Extend the four disposition options (e.g. consolidate, contain, replace in part) - Include costs of parallel operation, migration, archival and decommissioning in the investment case
